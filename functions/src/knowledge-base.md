# Giggre knowledge base

This file describes how the Giggre app works from the user's point of view.
Lines starting with [CHECK] still need to be confirmed by the Giggre team.

## About Giggre

Giggre is a hyperlocal gig marketplace. Hosts post short jobs ("gigs"). Workers nearby take them and get paid. The slogans are "Need Help? Giggre it!" and "Want Cash? Giggre it!"

One account can be used as both a worker and a host.

There are three kinds of gigs:
- Quick Gig: the app instantly matches the host with the nearest, most reliable worker who is online. Best for tasks that don't need special skills, like errands, moving items, or simple household tasks.
- Open Gig: posted publicly. Workers who have the required skill apply, and the host chooses who to select. Best for skilled work, like an electrician, plumber, or graphic designer.
- Offered Gig: sent directly to a worker the host already knows, usually a favorite or someone they hired before.

Where Giggre works:
- Gigs must be in the same country as you. A host can only post gigs in their own country, and a worker can't take a gig in another country.
- Money is shown in Philippine pesos (₱) if you are in the Philippines, and in US dollars ($) everywhere else. This is based on your location.
[CHECK] Which countries Giggre officially supports. The code has no country list; the sign-up phone picker offers 26 countries, but address search when posting a gig only suggests places in the Philippines.

Giggre is a mobile app (Android and iPhone). Everything in this guide happens in the app unless it says otherwise.
[CHECK] Whether any gig features (for example applying or chat) also work on the Giggre website. This codebase has no web version of the app; the only web page here is the referral invite page.

### Getting around: the main screen

After you log in you land on the main screen (the screen where you choose a mode; it is not the "Home" tab inside worker or host mode). It shows "Choose a mode to get started", with two options: "Gig Worker" (Find gigs & earn money) and "Gig Host" (Post gigs & find talent).

The top bar of the main screen has:
- the Giggre logo (opens a menu with About Giggre, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Help & FAQ, and Contact Us)
- the Gig Points button (your points balance)
- the person icon (your Profile)
- the wallet icon (your Gig Wallet)
- the log out icon

The main screen also shows "Giggre Updates" (news from Giggre; tap "See all" for more) and footer links: About, Terms, Privacy, Help, Contact Us.

## Worker mode and host mode

Worker mode is for finding and doing gigs. Host mode is for posting gigs and hiring workers.

To choose a mode:
1. On the main screen, tap "Gig Worker" or "Gig Host".
2. Tap "Continue as Gig Worker" or "Continue as Gig Host".

To switch modes while inside a mode:
1. Go to the Home tab of that mode.
2. Tap the round two-arrow (switch) icon in the top header. In host mode it is labelled "Switch to Worker Mode".

The switch icon is only inside a mode. If you are on the main screen, just tap "Gig Worker" or "Gig Host" and then the Continue button.

There are no restrictions on switching. You can switch even during an active gig.

Note: the "Gig Worker / Gig Host" toggle on the Profile tab does not switch modes. It only changes which stats and menu items the Profile tab shows.

Worker mode bottom tabs: Home, Saved, Chat, Profile.
Host mode bottom tabs: Home, My gigs, Post Gig (the round + button in the middle), Chat, Profile.

### Tutorials and demos

- Worker mode: a short tutorial plays the first time you open the dashboard. Replay it anytime with the graduation-cap icon in the dashboard header.
- Host mode: the gig-types tutorial plays the first time you tap "Post Gig". Replay it with the ▶ button that appears next to the Post Gig menu. Each posting form also has a "Tutorial" icon at the top.
- Video demos: Profile tab, then "Watch Demo". Choose the "Gig Worker" or "Gig Host" demo.

## Signing up and verification

### Creating an account

You can sign up with email, with Google, or with Apple (Apple only on iPhone and iPad).

To sign up with email:
1. Open the app and tap "Create account".
2. Fill in "Full Name" and your phone number. Tap the country code box to pick your country.
3. Fill in "Email Address", "Password", and "Confirm Password".
4. Optional: enter a friend's "Referral Code" (8 characters, like GX82KL19). If you came from an invite link, it may already be filled in.
5. Tick both boxes: "I confirm that I am of legal age and legally allowed to work and accept gigs." and "I have read and agree to Giggre's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."
6. Tap "Create account".

You will see "Welcome to Giggre! Your account is ready." There is no code sent by text or email; your account is ready right away.

Password rules: at least 8 characters, with 1 uppercase letter, 1 lowercase letter, 1 number, and 1 special character. A checklist under the password field shows which rules you've met.

To sign up with Google or Apple:
1. Tap "Create account" (or "Log In"), then tap "Google" or "Apple".
2. Choose your account.
3. On "Complete Your Profile", enter your Full Name and phone number, and an optional referral code.
4. Tick both agreement boxes.
5. Tap "Save & Continue".

Common sign-up errors:
- "All fields are required": fill in every field except Referral Code.
- "Please confirm that you are of legal age to continue." or "Please agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue.": tick both boxes.
- "Passwords do not match": type the same password in both fields.
- "That doesn't look like a valid ... phone number": check the number and the country code. Philippine numbers must be mobile numbers (starting with 09 or 9).
- "Referral code must be 8 characters" or "Invalid referral code": check the code, or leave the field empty.
- "This email is already registered. Try logging in instead."
- "This email is linked to a Google account. Log in with Google instead."
- "Registration did not finish. Please sign up again.": the sign-up was interrupted. Start again.

You can only enter a referral code when you sign up. It can't be added later.

### Logging in

1. Tap "Log In".
2. Enter your "Email Address" and "Password" and tap "Log In", or tap "Google" or "Apple".

Forgot your password:
1. On the Log In panel, type your email address.
2. Tap "Forgot password?".
3. Check your inbox for the reset email ("Password reset email sent! Check your inbox.").

If you see "Please enter your email address first.", type your email before tapping Forgot password.

Other login messages:
- "No account found with this email."
- "Incorrect password. Please try again."
- "Too many failed attempts. Please try again later."
- "This account has been disabled. Contact support for help."

### Verification

Verification shows hosts and workers that they can trust you. Verified users get a check badge on their profile.

Before you start, you must upload at least one document for each role in My Documents: one under "Gig Worker" and one under "Gig Host". See "My Documents" below.

To get verified:
1. Go to the Profile tab.
2. Under "Settings", tap "Verification".
3. Tap "Submit Verification Request".

The Giggre team reviews your profile and documents. Review usually takes 24 to 48 hours. You get a notification when you are approved ("Verification Approved") or rejected ("Verification Rejected").

The badge on the Verification row shows your status: Unverified, Pending, Verified, or Rejected.

If you are rejected, check your documents, then tap "Resubmit Verification Request".

While your request is pending, you can tap "Cancel Verification Request".

If you see "Unable to Submit", you haven't uploaded the documents for both roles yet. Tap "Go to My Documents".

After you log in, unverified users may see an "Account Not Verified" message. Tap "Verify Now" to start, or "Not Now" to skip.

What needs verification:
- Sending money from the Gig Wallet needs a verified account.
- In the current app, you can post, apply for, and accept gigs without being verified. On other people's profiles, unverified users show as "Unverified" or "Provisional".
[CHECK] Confirm that unverified users may post, apply for, and accept gigs. The approval message says "You can now take on gigs that require it", but the app has no gigs that require verification.

### My Documents

Profile tab, then "My Documents". Upload clear photos or PDFs.

Gig Worker documents: Valid ID (up to 2), Skill Certificates (up to 5), Resume / CV (1), Other Documents (up to 5).
Gig Host documents: Valid ID (up to 2), Business Certificate (up to 3), Business Permit (up to 2), Tax Documents (up to 3), Other Documents (up to 5).

To upload:
1. Tap the document type.
2. On "Share Your Document?", tap "I Agree".
3. Choose "Take Photo", "Choose from Gallery", or "Browse Files" (PDF, JPG, or PNG).

Tap a file to preview it. You can delete a document; this can't be undone.

If you see "Permission Denied", allow camera access for Giggre in your phone settings.

## For workers

### Your worker dashboard

The worker Home tab is the "Gig Worker Dashboard". From top to bottom it shows:
- Availability: shows "You're online · available for gigs" or "You're offline". When you are online, hosts nearby can see you and offer you gigs. When offline, you're hidden from hosts.
- Earned so far: your total earnings, gigs completed, and how much you earned this week (weeks start Monday).
- Work preferences: the "Quick Gigs" and "Auto Accept" switches (see "Quick Gigs for workers").
- Gigs Near You: a map or list of gigs.

The bell icon opens "Activity", which lists your gig updates from the last 30 minutes.

Giggre needs your location to show nearby gigs and match you with Quick Gigs. Location is required: while you are logged in, the app can't be used with location turned off or not allowed.
- If location services are off, you'll see "Turn on location". Tap "Turn on location" and switch location on.
- If Giggre isn't allowed to use your location, you'll see "Allow location access". Tap "Allow location" and choose to allow it. If it says "Open Settings", tap it, then go to Location and allow Giggre.
The app continues by itself once location is on. You can also tap "Log out" on that screen.

### Your skills (My Toolchest)

Some gigs need specific skills. Skills are awarded by the Giggre team.

To see or request skills:
1. Go to the Profile tab and tap "My Toolchest".
2. "My Skills" shows the skills you have.
3. To ask for a new skill, tap "New Skill Request", or open the "Browse" tab and tap "Request" next to a skill.
4. Fill in "Skill Name", "Skill Category", "Why do you want this skill added?", and your years or months of experience. Your "Experience Level" is set automatically from your experience.
5. Optional: add proof (PDF, JPG, or PNG), related work experience, a suggested requirement, and how to contact you for verification.
6. Tap "Submit Request".

You can follow your request in the "Request" tab. Statuses: Pending, Under Review, Needs More Info, Approved, Rejected. You get a notification when it is approved or rejected. If rejected, check the remarks in your Toolchest.

### Finding gigs

Open Gigs and Offered Gigs sent to you appear under "Gigs Near You" on the worker dashboard. Quick Gigs are not listed; they are sent to you as offers (see "Quick Gigs for workers").

1. Go to the worker Home tab.
2. Scroll to "Gigs Near You".
3. Tap "Map" or "List" to change the view.

On the map, tap the map first to move it ("Tap to interact with map"). Blue pins are Open Gigs and purple pins are gigs offered to you. Tap a pin to see the gig. Tap the fullscreen button for a bigger map.

In the list, use "Search gigs near you" to search by gig title or host name. Tap "See more" to load more gigs.

To filter gigs:
1. Tap the filter button next to Map/List.
2. "Show gigs": All Gigs, Matches My Skills, or Specific Skill.
3. "Distance": Any, 1 km, 5 km, 10 km, 50 km, or 100 km.
4. "Schedule": pick a date.
5. "Pay Type": All, Hourly, or Day (flat-rate gigs).
6. Tap "Done". Tap "Reset" to clear the filters.

By default the list shows gigs that match your skills, within 10 km. If you see no gigs, try "All Gigs" or a bigger distance.

You also get a "New Gig Nearby" notification when an Open Gig that matches your skills is posted within 10 km, if you are available for gigs.

Gigs from hosts you have blocked are hidden.

### The gig details

Tap a gig to open its details. You'll see the pay, an estimated work duration (an estimate only, not a commitment), the schedule, how many workers are needed, the location (tap "View map"), experience level, description, and the skills needed. Skills you have show in green.

Tap "Visit" to see the host's profile and rating.

### Saving a gig

1. Open the gig's details.
2. Tap the bookmark icon at the top.

Saved gigs are in the "Saved" tab. Tap the X on a saved gig to remove it. If a gig was removed, it shows "No longer available".

### Applying to an Open Gig

1. Open the gig's details.
2. Tap "Take Gig".
3. If the gig is 50 km or more away, you'll be asked to confirm. Tap "Take Gig Anyway" if you can travel there.
4. You'll see "Application submitted! Waiting for host selection."

The host gets a notification that you applied. There is no message or form to fill in.

After you apply, the gig shows an "Applied" badge. You can apply to more than one Open Gig at a time.

If the host selects you:
- You get a notification ("You're Assigned to a Open Gig!").
- If the app is open, you'll see "You got the gig!" with the shift, pay, location, and schedule.
- Tap "Go to location" to start (see "Check-in and doing the gig").

If you are not selected, you are not notified. The gig just disappears from your list, or shows "This gig is no longer available".

To withdraw your application:
1. Open the gig's details.
2. Tap "Withdraw", then "Withdraw" again.

You can withdraw anytime before the host selects you. You can apply again later if the gig is still open.

Why "Take Gig" might not work:
- The button is greyed out and you see "Missing skill": you don't have a skill the gig needs. Request it in My Toolchest.
- "This gig is out of reach": the gig is in a different country.
- "This gig is no longer available": the gig was filled, closed, or cancelled.
- "You've already put in for this gig."
- "You need to finish your current gig before taking another one.": you can only have one active gig at a time.
- A cancellation request is waiting for review (see "Cancellations").
- "Account Suspended": see "Quick Gigs for workers".

### Quick Gigs for workers

Quick Gigs are sent to one worker at a time. You don't apply; you get an offer.

To get Quick Gig offers:
1. On the worker Home tab, turn on the "Quick Gigs" switch ("Get instant offers while online"). This also makes you available.
2. Keep the Giggre app open in worker mode, with location on.

You're matched by how close you are, how often you accept offers, and your rating.

After you complete a Quick Gig, the Quick Gigs switch turns off. Turn it on again to get more offers.

When you get an offer:
1. A "Quick Gig Offer!" card appears at the bottom of the Home tab with the pay, place, distance, and a countdown.
2. Tap "Take It" to accept, or "Withdraw" to decline.

You have a short time to answer. The countdown shows how long. Usually it is 30 seconds. If time runs out, it counts as a decline.

[CHECK] The 30-second answer time is the default. The Giggre team can change it.

Declining matters:
- Declining or letting an offer expire lowers your acceptance rate. That makes you less likely to get offers.
- Each day you have a number of free declines. If you decline more than that, your account is suspended from Quick Gigs for a while. You'll see "Account Suspended" with the time you can accept gigs again.
- To see your declines and suspension status, go to Profile tab > "Settings" > "QUICK GIG STATUS". The "HOW GIGS ARE MATCHED" section shows the current limits and suspension rules.

Auto Accept: the "Auto Accept" switch on your dashboard automatically accepts Quick Gig offers for you.
[CHECK] The switch says "Auto-book gigs matching your skills", but in the app it accepts every Quick Gig offer without checking skills.

### Offered Gigs (direct offers)

A host can send a gig directly to you. You get a "New Gig Offer" notification, and a "Gig Offer for You!" card appears on your Home tab.

1. Read the offer on the card (pay, schedule, number of workers, skill, location).
2. Tap "I'm In" to accept, "No Thanks" to decline, or "Decide Later" to hide the card for now.

There is no countdown. If you decline, the host is notified. Declining an Offered Gig doesn't count against you.

You can also open the offer from the map or list and tap "I'm In". Declining is only on the card.

If you don't answer before the gig's scheduled time, the offer is cancelled automatically.

### Check-in and doing the gig

This part only works in the app, with location turned on.

After you are selected for or accept a gig, the gig screen opens with a map, your route, and the host's details. The steps are:

1. On My Way: go to the gig location. Your live location is shared with the host while you travel.
2. I'm Here: when you are within 40 meters of the gig location, you'll see "You're at the location — confirm your arrival." Tap "Confirm Arrival". The host is notified.
3. When you're ready to begin, tap "Start Gig". A timer starts and the host is notified.
4. On the Job: do the work.
5. When you're finished, tap "Gig Complete". The host is notified that the work is done.
6. Getting Paid: the host pays you (see "Getting paid").
7. Completed.

There is no separate check-in button. Arrival is detected by GPS. The "Confirm Arrival" button only appears when you are within 40 meters. If it doesn't appear:
- Make sure location (GPS) is on and Giggre has location permission. Messages like "Location is turned off. Tracking is paused." mean location is off.
- Move closer to the pinned gig location.

There are no photos to upload during a gig.

You can leave the gig screen; the gig keeps going. A bar at the bottom of the app shows "Gig in progress" and the current step. Tap "View" to go back.

To contact the host during the gig, use the voice call, video call, or chat buttons on the host card on the gig screen.

[CHECK] The step text on screen says the host confirms and starts the gig and marks it done, but the buttons ("Start Gig" and "Gig Complete") are on the worker's screen. This guide follows the buttons.

### Completing a gig

1. When the work is done, tap "Gig Complete" on the gig screen.
2. The host checks the work and pays you.
3. You confirm the payment with the host's code (see "Getting paid").
4. You'll see "Thank You!" and the amount you received. Tap "Continue".
5. Rate your host (see "Ratings").

The gig is completed only when the payment is confirmed. Finished gigs appear in Profile tab > "Gig History".

### Getting paid

Right now, hosts pay workers in cash, in person. Payment in the app (card, e-wallet, or Gig Wallet) is not available for gigs.

1. After you tap "Gig Complete", the host reviews the work and pays you in cash.
2. The host's phone shows a 6-digit payment code and a QR code.
3. On your phone, the "Confirm Payment" sheet opens. It shows the amount.
4. Tap "Scan QR" and scan the host's QR code, or type the 6-digit code.
5. Tap "Confirm Payment Received".

Only confirm after you have the cash.

If you closed the sheet, tap "Enter Payment Code" on the gig screen.

If you see "Incorrect code. Check the code with the host and try again.", ask the host to show the code again.

If the code doesn't work, the host can confirm the payment manually.

How the amount is set:
- Flat rate gigs: the agreed amount.
- Hourly gigs: the hourly rate times the time tracked between "Start Gig" and "Gig Complete", to the minute.
- The host can adjust the final amount before paying (for example, if the work ended early). The amount on your Confirm Payment sheet is the final amount.

If the payment is different from what was agreed, you can report the host (see "Account and safety").

Your earnings are shown in "Earned so far" on your dashboard and in "Gig History". If you've been paid in more than one currency, tap the (i) icon to see each currency separately.

Workers can also earn Gig Points for finished gigs (see "Gig Points").

### Your ratings

Profile tab (worker view) > "Ratings & Reviews" shows the ratings hosts gave you, your average, and a star breakdown.

## For hosts

### Your host dashboard

The host Home tab is the "Gig Host Dashboard". It shows:
- how many workers are online near you (within 10 km) for Quick Gigs
- a banner for each Open Gig with interested workers, with a "Review" button
- "Workers Near You": a map of online workers nearby
- "Active Gigs" and "Your Gigs" (tap "See all" for more)

Header icons:
- the bell opens "Activity" (your recent gig updates)
- the bookmark opens "Saved Templates"
- the switch icon is "Switch to Worker Mode"

The "My gigs" tab lists all your gigs. Filter by type (All Types, Quick Gig, Open Gig, Offered Gig) and by status (All Statuses, Active, Looking for a Worker, No Worker, Completed, Cancelled). Tap "Load More" to see more gigs.

Gig status labels you may see: Open, Offer Waiting, Looking for a Worker, No worker found, Underway, Partly Staffed, Fully Staffed, All Done, Cancellation requested, Completed, Cancelled, Declined.

### Posting a gig

You don't need to be verified or add a payment method to post a gig.
[CHECK] Confirm that unverified hosts may post gigs.

To start:
1. In host mode, tap the round "Post Gig" button at the bottom.
2. Choose "Quick Gig", "Open Gig", or "Offered Gig". Tap the ⓘ button if you want an explanation of each type.

Fields on all posting forms:
- Title (required): a short name for the task.
- Description (optional): details the worker should know.
- Pay Type: "Flat Rate" (one total amount, no matter how long it takes) or "Hourly Rate" (paid per hour, based on actual tracked time).
- Amount or Hourly Rate (required): must be more than 0. The currency is based on the gig's location.
- Work Duration (optional): roughly how long the gig will take. It's only a heads-up for workers, not a minimum or a commitment.
- Workers Needed (Quick and Open Gigs): 1 to 50. Each worker is paid the amount above separately.
- Schedule (required): tap "Pick a date" and "Pick a time". You can schedule up to a year ahead. If you don't pick a time, it's set to 8:00 AM.
- Location (required): your current GPS location is used. Tap "Select on Map" to choose another place, then "Confirm Location". On the map you can search an address, use your saved "Default Location" (set it with the star), or pick a "Recent" place.

Posting a Quick Gig:
1. Fill in the fields above.
2. Tap "Post Quick Gig".
3. The app starts looking for a worker right away (see "Quick Gig matching").

[CHECK] The search for a Quick Gig worker starts right away, even if you pick a later date. Confirm whether Quick Gigs are meant for "as soon as possible" only.

Posting an Open Gig:
1. Fill in the fields above.
2. Under "Required Skills", tap to pick the one skill the gig needs.
3. Choose the "Experience Level": Entry Level (no prior experience needed), Intermediate (1–3 years), or Expert (3+ years).
4. Tap "Post Open Gig".
5. You'll see "Open Gig Posted!". Nearby workers (within 10 km) who have that skill and are available get a notification.

Posting an Offered Gig:
1. Tap "Gig Worker" and choose the worker. Your favorite workers are listed. To find someone else, type their exact worker ID (3 letters and 6 digits, like ABC123456).
2. You can pick more than one worker. Tap "Use N selected worker(s)".
3. Under "Skill Required", pick a skill. Only skills the selected workers have are shown. If you picked several workers, they must share at least one skill.
4. Choose the "Experience Level" and fill in the other fields.
5. Tap "Send Offer".
6. You'll see "Offered Gig Sent!". The worker is notified.

Shortcut: on the "Workers Near You" map, tap a favorite worker and tap "Offer a Gig".

[CHECK] When an Offered Gig is sent to more than one worker, the workers do not seem to get a push notification. They only see the offer in the app.

Errors when posting:
- "Title is required", "Enter amount", "Enter a valid amount".
- "Schedule is required. Please pick a date and time." or "That time has already passed. Please choose a time in the future."
- "Location is required. Please enable GPS or select on map." Turn on location or pick a place on the map.
- "Outside Your Country": you can only post gigs in your own country.
- "Please select a required skill."
- "Please select at least one gig worker."
- "Content Not Allowed": the title or description has words that break the community guidelines. Change the text and try again.

You can't edit a gig after posting it. To change it, cancel it and post a new one, or use "Post Again" (see "Managing your gigs").

### Quick Gig matching

After you post a Quick Gig, a search screen shows "Searching nearby workers…" and a timer.

- The app offers the gig to the best worker nearby (online, available for Quick Gigs, within about 10 km). It looks at distance, how often they accept offers, and their rating.
- Each worker has a short time to accept. If they decline or don't answer, the next worker is offered the gig.
- When a worker accepts, you'll see "[name] confirmed". For multi-worker gigs, it shows how many have confirmed.
- If nobody accepts within about 5 minutes, the search stops with "No workers found nearby". The gig shows "No worker found".

If no worker is found, tap "Search again", or long-press the gig in "My gigs" and tap "Start New Search" or "Post Again".

To stop the search, tap "Cancel search", then "Cancel gig". This cancels the gig right away.

[CHECK] The 5-minute search time, 10 km distance, and answer time are defaults that the Giggre team can change.

### Choosing workers (Open Gigs)

You get a "New Application" notification each time a worker applies.

1. Tap the gig (or "Review" on the dashboard banner).
2. Under "INTERESTED WORKERS", see each worker's name, verification badge, rating, and gigs done.
3. Tap a worker to see their full profile: gigs completed, rating breakdown, relevant experience, and reviews.
4. Tap "Select" next to the worker you want, then "Select" again to confirm.
5. The worker is notified and heads to your location.

For gigs with more than one worker, keep tapping "Select" until all spots are filled. You can see "Spots filled" at the top. To stop taking more workers, tap "Close remaining spots".

There is no reject button. Workers you don't select are not notified. If you need to remove someone, you can block them from their profile.

If you don't select anyone before the scheduled time, the gig is cancelled automatically and you get a "Gig Cancelled" notification.

If some spots were never filled and all selected workers are done, you'll see "This gig didn't fill all its slots". Tap "Keep Looking" to choose a new date and accept more applicants, or "Mark Complete" to finish.

### Offered Gig responses

- If the worker accepts, the gig starts and you can follow it (see "Tracking a gig").
- If the worker declines, you get an "Offer Declined" notification. The gig shows "Declined". To offer the gig to someone else, create a new Offered Gig.
- If nobody answers before the scheduled time, the gig is cancelled automatically.

### Tracking a gig

This part only works in the app.

Tap an active gig to open it. You'll see a live map with the worker's location and the gig progress:

1. Worker's on the Way: the worker is heading to you. Their live location is shared.
2. Worker Arrived: you get a notification when the worker confirms they've arrived.
3. On the Job: you get a notification when the worker starts. A timer shows how long they've been working.
4. All Done: you get a notification when the worker marks the work as done. Check the work, then pay (see "Paying a worker").
5. Awaiting Payout: waiting for the worker to confirm the payment.
6. Completed.

You don't need to press anything to confirm arrival or start the gig. The worker does that from their phone.

You are not notified when a worker accepts a Quick Gig or Offered Gig. The gig status changes to "Underway".

To contact the worker, use the voice call, video call, or chat buttons on the worker card.

### Paying a worker and e-invoices

Right now, hosts pay workers in cash, in person. Card, e-wallet, and Gig Wallet payments for gigs are not available yet.

For a single-worker gig:
1. When the worker is done, open the gig and tap "Gig Complete".
2. Optional: tap the amount to open "Adjust Payment Amount". Enter the new amount and an optional reason, then tap "Save".
3. Under "Select Payment Method", choose "Cash" (Pay in person).
4. Tap "Confirm".
5. Pay the worker the cash.
6. Show the worker your "Payment Code": a 6-digit code and a QR code. The worker scans it or types it in to confirm they were paid.
7. When the worker confirms, you'll see "Gig Complete!". Then rate the worker (see "Ratings").

For a gig with several workers, each worker has their own card. Tap "Mark Complete & Pay" on each worker's card, then follow the same steps.

For hourly gigs, the amount is your hourly rate times the time tracked, to the minute.

If you closed the payment code screen, tap "Show Payment Code" on the gig.

If the code can't be used (for example, the worker's phone is off), tap "Confirm Manually". Use this only if the worker has told you they received the payment.

Payment history: Profile tab (host view) > "Payment History" shows what you've paid, per gig and per worker.

### Gig Points

Workers earn Gig Points for finished gigs. Hosts don't earn points.

How you earn them:
1. Finish the gig and tap "Gig Complete".
2. The host approves the work. The reward is locked in and shows as "Approved · processing".
3. When the payment is confirmed, the points are "Credited" to your available balance. You get a "+N Gig Points" notification.

How many points you get depends on the gig type. Some gigs have their own reward, shown on the gig. Gigs that are cancelled, get no worker, or are declined earn no points.

To see your points:
1. Tap the "Gig Points" button in the top bar of the main screen.
2. You'll see your "Available balance", points still "Processing", and your lifetime total.
3. The "How to earn" tab shows the points for each gig type. (Open Gigs are called "Posted Gig" there.)

Converting points:
1. On the Gig Points screen, tap "To GigCash" (points into Gig Wallet money) or "To Points" (Gig Wallet money into points).
2. Enter the number of points. The screen shows the rate and what you receive or what it costs.
3. Optional: add a note.
4. Tap "Review conversion", then "Send request".

An admin reviews each conversion. Your points or GigCash are put on hold until it's decided. You're notified when it's approved or rejected. If rejected, your points or GigCash come back.

Rules:
- You can only have one conversion request waiting at a time.
- There are minimum, maximum, and daily limits. Daily limits reset at midnight Philippine time.
- You can cancel a waiting request from the "Requests" tab.

If you see "Gig Points rewards are paused right now", no new points are being given for now. Points you already have are safe.

[CHECK] Point values, conversion rates, and limits are set by the Giggre team and are not listed here.

### Gig Wallet

The Gig Wallet holds GigCash, Giggre's in-app money.

To open it, tap the wallet icon in the top bar of the main screen.

The wallet screen shows your "Available GigCash" and three buttons: "Top Up", "Withdraw", and "Send". The tabs are "Activity" (your transactions), "Requests" (your top up and send requests), and "How it works".

Gig payments do not use the wallet. Hosts pay workers in cash (see "Getting paid").

Topping up (adding GigCash):
1. Tap "Top Up".
2. Choose how to pay. Available methods can include "Cash at office" (pay at a Giggre office), "Remittance center" (Palawan, Cebuana, M Lhuillier and other padala centers), and GCash or Maya when Giggre has set them up. Methods marked "coming soon" can't be used yet.
3. Enter the amount. The minimum and maximum are shown.
4. Pay using the details shown (Giggre's account number, office address, or remittance receiver).
5. Enter the reference number, official receipt (OR) number, or control number from your payment. You can add a photo or screenshot of the receipt.
6. Tap "I've paid — submit for review".

Giggre checks your payment and adds the GigCash. You're notified when it's approved ("Top up approved") or rejected. Nothing is added until it's approved.

Sending GigCash to another user:
1. Tap "Send". Your account must be verified.
2. Enter the person's Philippine mobile number and tap "Send to ...".
3. Enter the amount and an optional message.
4. Check the details and tap "Send request".

The amount is taken from your wallet right away and held. An admin reviews every send. When it's approved, the other person receives the money and you're notified. If it's rejected or you cancel it, the money comes back to your wallet.

Rules:
- You can only have one top up or send request waiting at a time. To make a new one, wait until it's decided or cancel it in the "Requests" tab.
- Each request has a minimum, a maximum, and a daily limit. Daily limits reset at midnight Philippine time.
- To cancel a waiting request, open the "Requests" tab and tap "Cancel". If you already paid for a top up, contact support instead of cancelling.
- If you send to someone whose wallet uses another currency, they receive the amount at the current exchange rate.

Withdrawing (cashing out to GCash or a bank) is not available yet. It's coming soon.

Fees: Giggre doesn't charge a fee for top ups or sends. Remittance centers charge their own fee.
